Getting started

CS Agent is a workspace you keep open all day - like Gmail. Your 7am digest is waiting when you arrive, and signals surface in the chat interface in real time throughout the day.

Create your account

  1. Go to cs-agent.app and click Sign up.
  2. Sign in with your Google account or create an account with your email address.
  3. Your 14-day free trial starts immediately. No credit card required.

Connect your tools during onboarding

After signing up, CS Agent walks you through connecting your Google Workspace and CRM. Connecting these during onboarding ensures your first digest is ready the next morning at 7am.

You can also connect or reconnect integrations at any time from Settings.

Google Workspace

CS Agent uses Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive (for Meet transcripts) to build your daily digest and surface proactive signals.

Connect Google Workspace

  1. Go to Settings in CS Agent.
  2. Under Connections, find the Google Workspace section.
  3. Click Connect Google and complete Google's OAuth consent screen.
  4. CS Agent requests read access to Gmail metadata, Google Calendar, and Google Drive Meet artifacts, plus permission to send email on your behalf when you approve a draft. No email bodies are accessed.

What CS Agent reads from Gmail: Subject lines, sender and recipient addresses, and timestamps only. CS Agent never receives or stores email bodies. This restriction is enforced at the API level using Gmail's metadata-only format.

Reconnect Google Workspace

If your Google connection expires or is revoked, CS Agent will show a Reconnect option in Settings. Click it and complete the OAuth flow to restore access.

Disconnect Google Workspace

You can revoke CS Agent's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This removes the access token immediately. Previously derived signals stored in CS Agent are subject to the retention rules in the Privacy Policy.

CRM - HubSpot and Gainsight

CS Agent supports HubSpot and Gainsight as CRM and CSP sources. Connect one or both to surface account context alongside your Google signals.

Connect HubSpot

  1. Go to Settings and find the HubSpot row under Connections.
  2. Click Connect HubSpot and complete HubSpot's OAuth consent screen.
  3. CS Agent reads account records, contact details, deal stages, and open tasks from HubSpot. No data is written to HubSpot without your explicit approval of a draft action.

Connect Gainsight

  1. Go to Settings and find the Gainsight row under Connections.
  2. Click Connect Gainsight and follow the Gainsight OAuth flow.
  3. CS Agent reads account health scores, CTAs, and timeline entries. No writes happen without your approval.

Disconnect a CRM

  1. Go to Settings and find the CRM row you want to disconnect.
  2. Click Disconnect next to that CRM.
  3. CS Agent removes the access token and clears the cached account snapshot for that CRM. If you have a second CRM connected, its snapshot is not affected.
  4. You can reconnect at any time using Connect a CRM in Settings.

Slack

The Slack integration lets CS Agent draft Slack messages for your approval and surface unresponded Slack threads mentioning your accounts.

What CS Agent does with Slack

  • Outbound drafts: Ask CS Agent to draft a Slack message to a teammate or channel. CS Agent shows you the draft first. You review, edit if needed, and approve. Nothing is sent until you approve.
  • Inbound signals: CS Agent monitors for Slack threads mentioning your customer accounts that haven't received a response. These surface as signals in your digest and chat interface.

Privacy: CS Agent reads only thread metadata and message content needed to identify unresponded mentions of your accounts. Raw message content is processed in memory and not stored in CS Agent's database.

Connect Slack

  1. Go to Settings and find the Slack row under Connections.
  2. Click Connect Slack and complete Slack's OAuth consent screen.
  3. CS Agent requests a user token to search messages and send messages on your behalf when you approve a draft.

Use Slack in CS Agent

To draft a Slack message, open CS Agent's chat and ask - for example: "Draft a Slack to Marcus in the Meridian channel letting him know the QBR is confirmed for Thursday." CS Agent produces a draft with the intended recipient and message text. Click Edit to revise or Approve to send.

Unresponded Slack threads mentioning your accounts appear as signals in your daily digest and can surface in chat when relevant.

Disconnect Slack

  1. Go to Settings and find the Slack row.
  2. Click Disconnect.
  3. CS Agent removes the Slack access token immediately. CS Agent will no longer send Slack messages or monitor Slack signals for your account.
  4. To fully revoke access, you can also remove the CS Agent app from your Slack workspace at Slack > Settings > Integrations > Installed apps.

Zoom

The Zoom integration gives CS Agent access to transcripts from your customer calls, enabling post-call recaps and unrecapped-call alerts for meetings recorded in Zoom.

What CS Agent does with Zoom

  • Post-call recaps: After a recorded Zoom call with a customer, CS Agent retrieves the transcript, generates a recap, and drafts a CRM timeline entry for your review. Nothing is logged until you approve the draft.
  • Unrecapped-call alerts: If CS Agent detects a customer call that has a recording but no recap, it surfaces this in your digest so nothing slips through.
  • Pre-call context: When you have an upcoming Zoom call with a customer, CS Agent can surface account context and prior call notes to prepare you.

Privacy: Zoom transcript content is processed in memory at the moment of use and is never persisted to CS Agent's database. Only the AI-generated recap and draft timeline entry are stored - not the source transcript. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Requirement: Zoom AI Companion (Smart Recording or Meeting Summary) must be enabled on your Zoom account for transcript retrieval to work. This is an account-level setting managed by your Zoom admin. If transcripts aren't available, CS Agent will note this and still surface the unrecapped-call signal.

Connect Zoom

  1. Go to Settings and find the Zoom row under Connections.
  2. Click Connect Zoom and sign in to your Zoom account on Zoom's OAuth consent screen.
  3. Approve the requested permissions: CS Agent needs access to read your cloud recordings and retrieve meeting transcripts.
  4. Once connected, CS Agent will begin surfacing post-call signals and alerts for recorded customer meetings.

Use Zoom in CS Agent

No manual action is needed after connecting. CS Agent monitors for new recordings in the background. When a recorded call with a customer account is detected:

  1. CS Agent retrieves the Zoom transcript.
  2. A post-call recap and draft CRM timeline entry appear in your CS Agent workspace.
  3. Review the draft. Click Edit to revise or Approve to log it to your CRM.

You can also ask CS Agent about a recent call in the chat interface - for example: "What did we commit to in yesterday's Northgate call?"

Note: Account matching uses the Zoom meeting topic. Calls with generic titles like "Weekly sync" may not be automatically matched to an account. If a call isn't matched, it will still surface as an unrecapped-call alert so you can handle it manually.

Disconnect Zoom

  1. Go to Settings and find the Zoom row.
  2. Click Disconnect.
  3. CS Agent removes the Zoom access token immediately. CS Agent will no longer retrieve Zoom transcripts or surface Zoom-based signals for your account.
  4. To fully revoke access, you can also remove the CS Agent app from your Zoom account at marketplace.zoom.us/user/installed.

Zoom data and privacy

CS Agent's Zoom integration handles transcript data with the following protections:

  • Transcripts are retrieved over an encrypted HTTPS connection using your personal Zoom OAuth token.
  • Transcript content is processed in memory during recap generation and is not written to CS Agent's database.
  • Only the AI-generated recap and timeline entry draft are stored, not the source transcript text.
  • Your Zoom OAuth token is encrypted at rest using AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256 (Fernet) and is stored only in CS Agent's database, never on disk.
  • Transcript content may be included in a prompt sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate the recap. Under CS Agent's contract with Anthropic, Anthropic does not use your data to train their models.

Data and privacy

CS Agent is built around data minimization. We access only what is needed, process sensitive content in memory without persisting it, and give you full control over your data.

Delete your data

You can delete all your CS Agent data immediately from Settings > Data and privacy > Delete my data. This permanently removes your account and all associated data across all integrations. This action cannot be undone.

Cancel your subscription

Canceling your CS Agent subscription triggers a complete data purge within 30 days, per our Privacy Policy.
