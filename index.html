CS Agent

An AI assistant for Customer Success Managers.

CS Agent connects to a CSM's email, calendar and CRM, and surfaces the signals that need attention: accounts that have gone quiet, renewals coming up, meetings to prepare for. It drafts the next step, and nothing is sent or written without the CSM's approval.

  • Morning digest of what needs attention today
  • Pre-call context and post-call follow-up drafts
  • Chat over your accounts, contacts and recent activity

To run your own instance, see the README in this project's source repository for setup and configuration.
